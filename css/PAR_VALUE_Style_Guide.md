# Par Value — Brand Style Guide

*Last updated: June 24, 2026*

The trusted, fairly-priced, golfer-to-golfer marketplace. The name carries a dual meaning — **par** as the golf benchmark, **par value** as the finance term for fair face value — and the brand should always feel like the intersection of the two: athletic credibility plus financial trust.

---

## Logo

**Wordmark:** A stacked serif lockup — `PAR` on the top line, `VALUE` on the bottom line, with a thin green horizontal rule (the "par-line") set between them.

- The par-line is **inset and centered** — it never runs edge to edge. It reads as a divider between the two words, not as an underline of `PAR`.
- App icon variant uses the same inset par-line (not full-width).
- Variants produced: primary, reversed, horizontal, and app icon.

**Do not** use literal golf iconography — no flags, balls, tees, or club silhouettes. The brand leans premium/financial, not novelty-golf.

## Color

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Fairway green (light backgrounds) | `#1A7F5A` | Primary brand green |
| Fairway green (on dark) | `#35A87D` | Use on dark backgrounds — the par-line, URL, accents |
| Deep green-charcoal (dark bg) | `#0D1511` | Primary dark background for cards |
| Warm off-white | `#F5F2EA` | Primary text on dark; softer than pure white |
| Muted green | `#789E8C` | Secondary/supporting text labels |
| Frame line | `#26382F` | Subtle inset border ("certificate" cue) |

The thin inset frame on dark cards is a deliberate "we set the standard" signal — it reads like a certificate / statement of value. Use it sparingly and keep it subtle.

---

## Typography

| Use | Typeface | Treatment |
|-----|----------|-----------|
| Wordmark & display | **Playfair Display** | Serif, weight ~500. High-contrast Didone — premium, editorial, financial |
| Supporting lines, labels, URL | **Montserrat** | Uppercase, letter-tracked. Clean geometric sans |

- Keep the typography to **these two families only**. Avoid introducing additional styles (e.g. italic display) unless a specific campaign calls for it.
- Supporting lines and the URL are set in **tracked uppercase Montserrat** (added letter spacing).

---

## URL Lockup

`GETPARVALUE.COM` — Montserrat, tracked uppercase, in fairway green (`#35A87D` on dark). Typically anchored at the bottom of a card.

---

## Layout Principles

- **Premium minimal.** Generous negative space; let the wordmark breathe as the hero.
- Center-balanced compositions on a 1:1 (1080×1080) format for primary social posts.
- Hierarchy on a launch/brand card: wordmark (hero) → slogan/supporting line → URL.
